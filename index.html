About

The short version

I started in regular full-stack work, and kept getting pulled toward one question: how much does an app really need to know about its users?

Zero-knowledge proofs answer it elegantly, so I moved much of my work to the Midnight Network, where I build dApps, circuits and the tooling around them.

I like problems that are slow, boring and in everyone's way. A gap in the docs is one of them, so I write the guide, or send the pull request.

I also serve as welfare secretary of my alumni association, which keeps me honest about building things people actually use.

who I build for

Businesses and NGOs in Abuja that need a real site or tool, and dev-tool and Web3 teams that need clear docs.
